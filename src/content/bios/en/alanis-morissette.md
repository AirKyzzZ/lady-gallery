Alanis Morissette grew up in Ottawa. Her father taught French, and her twin brother Wade is also a musician. As a child she appeared on the kids' sketch show *You Can't Do That on Television*, and in the early 1990s she released two dance-pop albums in Canada.

Then she moved to Los Angeles and met producer Glen Ballard, who let her use his studio and write her own way. *Jagged Little Pill* came out in June 1995 on Maverick, Madonna's label. It was only expected to sell enough to justify a second album. Then KROQ, an influential Los Angeles rock station, started playing *You Oughta Know*, a furious break-up song with Flea and Dave Navarro of the Red Hot Chili Peppers. *Hand in My Pocket*, *You Learn* and *Ironic*, her biggest hit, followed. The album won her five Grammys, including Album of the Year, and she was the youngest winner of that award at the time.

From *Under Rug Swept* (2002) she produced her records alone. In 2018 *Jagged Little Pill* became a stage musical, which went on to win two Tony Awards on Broadway. In 2026 she was inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame.

Play *Jagged Little Pill* in order, starting with *You Oughta Know*. For a different mood, try *Uninvited*, written for the *City of Angels* soundtrack in 1998.
